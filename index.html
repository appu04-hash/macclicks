About

Hi, I'm Appu. I chase good light.

I'm the photographer behind Mac Clicks, a freelance studio built on one belief: the best pictures feel like a memory. Whether it's a wedding, a portrait session, a street corner at dusk or a product on a clean table, I look for the calm, natural moment and let it breathe.

My style is minimal, moody and true to life — natural light, honest expressions and a gentle, unhurried approach on set so you can simply be yourself.
